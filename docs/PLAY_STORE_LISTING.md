# Pulse — Med Study Tracker

## Short description

Plan realistic study time around rotations and track your Step 2 CK progress.

## Full description

Pulse helps medical students make steady progress without pretending every day has unlimited time.

Use Pulse to:

- See a realistic study block around clinical shifts and lectures
- Protect recovery after long rotation days
- Track progress across Step 2 CK topics
- Log focused study sessions in seconds
- Review your weekly schedule at a glance
- Keep your study plan simple and actionable

Pulse is a planning and tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, or exam content.
