# Privacy Policy for Pulse — Med Study Tracker

**Effective date:** September 15, 2026

Pulse — Med Study Tracker is designed to work without an account and without sending study data to a server.

## Information stored by the app

Pulse stores the study information you enter, such as topic progress, completed schedule blocks, and logged study minutes, locally on your device. This information is used only to display your study plan and progress inside the app.

## Information we collect

The first release of Pulse does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not use advertising identifiers, analytics tracking, or third-party login.

## Sharing

Because study data stays on the device in the first release, Pulse does not share that data with us or with third parties.

## Data deletion

You can remove the app from your device to delete its locally stored data. If you need help, contact medstudytracker@gmail.com.

## Children

Pulse is intended for adult medical students and trainees. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds accounts, cloud backup, analytics, advertising, or payments, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled.
